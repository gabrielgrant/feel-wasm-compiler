## Caveats

- Headless Chromium on a single machine; no CPU pinning or frequency control. Repeat runs to gauge variance.
- feelin evaluates with binary floating point; equivalence was checked per workload and non-equivalent workloads are excluded from the speed comparison rather than compared.
- feelin is measured through its public API, which re-parses the expression on each call; a compile-once interpreter would be faster than shown. Section 5 shows our cost when compilation is not amortized.
- Cold-start fetch times are over localhost; real networks add download time for ~5 MB of uncompressed Wasm (compressible).
